# Horizon Nav settings

Every setting in the app, in the order it appears in **Settings**, with what it does. Names are the app's English names. You can
also find any of them with the search box at the top of Settings.

Some settings appear only in one edition: the Google Play edition (Horizon Maps) has no updater, donations or "Not showing in
Android Auto?" help; the GitHub edition has no "Song and artwork".

## Map style
*Colours, day and night, map detail*

**Day and night**
- **Day and night**: when the map switches between its day and night look.
  - On a phone: *Auto* (sunrise and sunset, with dawn and dusk light between), *Phone's dark mode*, *My times*, or always *Day* or *Night*.
  - On a head unit: *Auto* (the head unit's own night mode once it has switched, sun times until then), *Headlights* (follows the head unit's night mode when it switches with your lights), *Sun times*, or *My times*.
- **Lights signal is reversed**: turn on if the head unit says your lights are on when they're off.
- **Night starts at** / **Day starts at**: for *My times*, on a 24-hour clock (for example 19:30 and 06:45).

**Colours and lettering**
- The map's colour theme: pick one from the pictures, or make your own under *Custom colours*.
- **Car's lighting colour**: the map, mostly at night, takes a hint of your car's interior lighting colour. **How strong** sets how much.
- **Font**: the lettering for road and place names.

**Custom colours / Your themes**
- Build your own theme: **Starts from** (the theme your colours go on top of), colours for the map's parts, **Route line width**, **Night brightness**, and a **Name** to keep it under *Your themes*.

**The map**
- **Map source**: *Mapbox* (the 3D map), *TomTom* (TomTom's map in Horizon colours, needs a TomTom key) or *OpenStreetMap*. All need the Mapbox token, as Mapbox's engine draws them.
- **Map detail**: *Full 3D* (buildings, landmarks, trees and shadows), *Lite* (flat buildings, no landmarks or shadows) or *Lightest* (Mapbox's simpler 2D map, for slow head units).
- **Default zoom**: your zoom when not on a route. Or zoom with + and − on the map and tap *Make this my default*.
- **Auto zoom**: zooms out at speed and in at junctions. Off keeps one steady zoom, which + and − still change.
- **Footpaths and trails**: turn off for a cleaner driving map.
- **HD roads**: lane markings, turn arrows and 3D overpasses where Mapbox has them (Full 3D). *Automatic* is on in the UK only, as in some countries they hide ordinary streets.

## Light & 3D
*Light, shadows, map view, 3D, sky*

**Light**
- **Night brightness**: how bright the whole night map is. 100% is Mapbox's own night light.
- **Moonlight at night**: a low blue moonlight with long soft shadows, in place of Mapbox's flat night light. **Moon strength** sets how strongly it lights walls and casts shadows.
- **Shadows**: buildings and trees cast shadows. Off is also lighter work for a slow head unit.
- **Sun in its real place**: light and shadows come from where the sun really is right now (and the moon at night).

**Map buttons**: which buttons show on the map (Compass, Report, and while navigating Sound, Other routes, Clean view and Overview). Map options is always there.

**Map view**
- **Tilt on a wide screen** / **on a tall screen** / **on Android Auto**: how steep the 3D view is on each kind of screen. Higher shows further ahead (more for a slow unit to draw).
- **Flatten the map to 2D at turns**: the view eases down to a flat map at junctions and roundabouts, then tilts back up.
- **Tilt with speed**: flatter when slow, a longer view at speed.
- **Traffic overview when stopped**: after 30 seconds stopped, the map pulls back to a top-down view of the traffic round you.
- **Circle the car when stopped**: instead, the camera slowly circles your car after you've been stopped a while (**Start after** 15 s, 30 s or 1 min; **Circling speed** slow or medium; **Direction**), and comes back round when you move off. More work for a slow head unit.
- **Turn card colour**: the colour behind the next turn (the writing stays white), or *Match road signs*: the colour of the country's signs for the road you're on, motorway colour on motorways.

**3D**
- **Hill shading**: soft shading on hillsides, even on a flat map. Lighter work than 3D hills.
- **3D buildings**, **Detailed building fronts** (windows, doors and shopfronts, mostly city centres), **3D landmarks** (well-known buildings, bridges and stadiums), **3D hills and valleys** (real terrain, harder work for a slow head unit), **Trees in parks**.
- **Keep the road ahead clear**: 3D trees and landmarks are left out round your arrow and along the road just ahead, so they never hide it.
- **Tap to highlight**: tap a building to light it up; tap a shop or place, then hold on it to go there.

**Sky and satellite**
- **Satellite**: satellite pictures instead of the drawn map.
- **Night sky in 3D**: twinkling stars above the horizon after dark.

## Weather
*Live weather, rain, snow, fog, radar, forecasts*

- **Live weather**: checks the weather where you are every 10 minutes. Everything below needs it.
- **Rain on the map**, **Snow on the map**: falling rain or snow, heavier in a downpour or storm.
- **Fog and mist on the map**: a pale wash and a thick haze in fog or mist. Off shows them as a plain overcast day.
- **Haze towards the horizon**: the sky matches the weather (grey when overcast, thicker in rain).
- **Weather advice**: a quiet note in heavy rain, snow, fog or ice when you're near the limit.
- **Overcast light**: a greyer, softer daylight with no hard shadows when it's raining or snowing.
- **Weather on the route**: the forecast along your route, for when you'll be there.
- **Rain radar layer**: live radar from RainViewer, under the route.

## On the map
*Traffic, lights, cameras, signs, lamps*

- **What's here**: holding on the map shows the road, its speed limit and the area first, with *Go* and *Add as a stop*. Off goes straight there.
- **Saved places on the map**: Home, Work, favourites and starred places; tap one to go there. **Saved places icons** picks their look.

**Traffic**
- **Live traffic**: traffic colours on the roads.
- **Only show traffic on your route while navigating**: off shows traffic on the roads around you too.
- **Light traffic in green**: free-flowing main roads shown green, as well as slow traffic in amber and red.
- **Blend traffic colours**: on the route line and the trip card's route bar, the colours run into each other instead of changing in steps.

**On your route**
- **Traffic lights**: the ones on your route.
- **Speed cameras and roadworks**: their icons on the map (the warnings are under [Alerts](#alerts)). **Speed camera size** sets the camera badge's size, and **Hazard sign size** the roadworks, closure and hazard signs'. **Limit sign size on the route** sets the speed limit signs drawn where the limit changes.
- **Zebra crossings**: crossings painted on the road, with Belisha beacons at zebras: on your route, and round you in towns when there's no route.
- **Stop signs**: STOP and Give Way signs on your route, with a short chime before STOP signs (where OpenStreetMap has them).
- **Number-plate cameras**: Flock and other automatic number-plate cameras from OpenStreetMap. Map only, no alerts.

**3D street furniture**
- **3D street lamps, traffic lights and cameras**: real-size 3D models with lit lamps (Full 3D). Off shows simpler badges.
- **Street lamps** and **Street lamp spacing**.

## Your arrow & route line
*Arrow or car, colours, route look*

**Your arrow**
- **Model shape**: an arrow, a 3D car, a 3D model file of your own, or a picture.
- For a car: **Car type** (saloon, hatchback, estate, SUV, pickup, van, sports), its colour, **Model size**, **Glow** (a soft blue glow, the model lit brightly).
- For a picture: **Picture** (a PNG with a see-through background looks best; point its top the way you drive), **Turns with you**, **Remove white background**, **Round crop**.
- **Headlight beam at night**: a soft cone of light on the road ahead after dark, while you're moving.

**Where your arrow sits**
- **Keep the arrow on the road with no route**: it snaps to the road you're on.
- **Keep the arrow to the side**: when the cards fold away, instead of moving to the middle.

**Route line**
- Its colours, plus **3D flags** (a tall flag at your destination and smaller ones at stops), **Turn arrow on the route** (a white arrow through the next junction), **Chevrons along the route**, **Route ribbon** (a soft shadow under the line, and the route showing faintly through buildings) and **Flowing light along the route**.

## Routes
*Vehicle, avoid, choosing, starting*

**Vehicle**
- **Vehicle**: car, van or lorry. For a van or lorry, routes avoid low bridges, weight limits and lorry bans for your **Height** (including the load), **Width**, **Length**, **Weight**, **Axle weight** and **Hazardous goods** (avoids tunnels and roads closed to hazardous loads).
- **Top speed for arrival times**: routes are timed no faster than this, for vans, lorries or towing.

**Avoid**
- **Avoid toll roads**, or **Avoid tolls only in** chosen countries.
- **Avoid motorways**: *Never*, *Always*, or *Short trips*, when where you're going is less than a distance you choose away (**Short trips: under**, 10 to 50 miles or 15 to 80 km).
- **Avoid ferries**, **Avoid car trains** (like the Channel Tunnel shuttle), **Avoid unpaved roads**.
- **Avoid Clean Air Zones and ULEZ** (a zone you start or finish in can't be avoided), and **Clean air zones on this unit** (the zone data kept on the device).

**Choosing a route**
- **Eco routes**: fuel-efficient instead of fastest.
- **Prefer simpler routes**: a route only a minute or so slower with far fewer turns is picked first (labelled *Simplest*).
- **Prefer main roads**: keeps to main roads rather than residential streets and lanes, even if a few minutes slower (labelled *Main roads*). Online it picks from the routes Mapbox offers (not TomTom's); offline car routes favour bigger roads.
- **Offer faster routes**: tells you when a faster route turns up on the way.
- **Keep other routes on the map**: grey lines with +/− minutes you can tap any time. Off shows a *Faster route found* card instead.
- **Suggest where you're going**: guesses your destination, with the live journey time, including home and work at your usual times.
- **Arrive on your side of the road**: brings you to the kerb side of your destination, so you don't cross the traffic (Mapbox and offline routes).

**Starting a route**
- **Auto-start route countdown** and **Auto-start countdown**: start navigation by itself after a countdown.
- **Fly along a new route**: a 3-second 3D fly-through before the overview (tap the map to skip).
- **Fly over route button**: on the route options, a 3D flight along the whole drive.

## Turn card & lanes
*Signs, lanes, trip card, arriving*

**Turn card**
- **Road signs on the turn card**: the road number and the exit number as small signs.
- **Living turn card**: grows and glows as the turn gets close.
- **Lane guidance on the turn card**, and **Lane guidance style**: a big lane picture before junctions with two or more lanes, or the small lane strip at every junction.
- **Next turn on the turn card**: the turn after the next one, only when it comes straight after, or all the time. **Shown as** picks where.

**Lanes and junctions**
- **Junction pictures**: Mapbox's pictures of motorway junctions in the last kilometre, where Mapbox has them and offers them to your account.
- **Lanes on the map**: your lanes lit on the road.
- **Zoom in on lanes before a junction**.
- **Junction close-up**: at slip roads, exits, forks and lane splits the view moves closer and a little flatter for the last 200 m.
- **Junction zoom**: *Further*, *Usual*, *Closer* or *Closest*: how close the map comes in for turns, lanes and junctions. Further keeps more of the roads round a turn in view; closer makes the lit lanes bigger on a small screen.

**Trip card**
- **Route bar on the trip card**: traffic, cameras, services and stops, with your progress.
- **Next services card**.
- **Hazard radar**: bends, crossings, schools, bumps, cameras and checks ahead.

**Arriving**
- **Arrival moment**: the camera swoops to your destination and shows the nearest parking.
- **Light up your destination**: the building glows, its entrance is marked and a dotted line shows the walk from the parking.
- **Parking near your destination**: car parks close by, with the walk from each, before you arrive.
- **Route back**: one tap on the arrival card for the way back.

**Other screens**
- **Next turn in the notification**: the arrow, distance and road in the app's notification, for car launchers, watches and other screens.

## Alerts
*Speed, cameras, hazards, reports*

**Speed**
- **Warn when over the limit**, by how much (**Warn when over by**, **Measure as**, separately **In towns**, up to 60 km/h / 40 mph, and **Outside towns**).
- **Flash the sign when over**: the limit sign flashes while you're over by more than this.
- **Speed limit sounds**: the beep when you go over and the sound when the limit changes. Off keeps them quiet while other alerts still sound.
- **Say "Slow down"**: after the beep when you go over the limit.
- **Speed alert sound**: the sound for going over, so it can't be mistaken for camera and hazard beeps.
- **Speed alert in the background**: keeps the speed alert going while another app is in front, even with no route (uses more battery).
- **Top speed alert**: a beep when you reach a speed you choose, whatever the limit. **Say "Slow down" at top speed** speaks only then.
- **Say when the limit changes** and **How** (chime, voice or both).
- **Pop-up when the limit changes**: the banner with the new limit. Off leaves just the sign by your speedometer.
- **Upcoming speed limits**: the next limit on your route, on the map and as a small sign with its distance, from about half a mile away.
- **Show estimated limits**: where no limit is mapped, the usual one for that kind of road (greyed out). Off shows no sign.

**Cameras**
- **Speed cameras**: spoken and on-screen warnings.
- **Off where they're banned**: switches off by itself in France, Germany and Switzerland, which don't allow camera warnings.
- **Average speed checks**: shows your average against the limit.

**Hazards**
- **Roadworks and incidents**, and **Roadworks warnings** (off stops the roadworks warning line and voice; accidents and closures still warn).
- **Wrong-way warning**: if you turn the wrong way into a one-way street.
- **Speed bumps**, **Sharp bends** (striped yellow on the route, with a comfortable speed before each), **Schools at school-run times**, **Level crossings and fords**.

**Zones and reminders**
- **Clean Air Zones and ULEZ**: a warning before you enter one.
- **Break reminder after 2 hours**.

**Your reports**
- **Warn about reports ahead**: hazards you or others reported.
- **Reports still active**: the reports you've made.

## Sound & voice
*Spoken directions and volume*

**Directions**
- **Language**: the whole app, directions and Android Auto. *Phone's language* uses English when the phone's isn't one of these. **Missing your language?** asks for it on GitHub.
- **Spoken guidance**: all, or alerts only (cameras, limits and hazards, no turn-by-turn), or off.
- **Say road names**: off gives just "In 200 metres, turn left".
- **Landmarks in directions**: "At the traffic lights, turn right" when the turn is at lights or a stop sign.
- **Smarter directions**: junction numbers and sign destinations, the next move when it follows straight after, and fewer early prompts on roads you drive a lot.

**Voice and volume**
- **Voice channel**: if you hear the beeps but not the voice, choose *Media* (some head units don't play the navigation channel). If the voice shares the music's volume, try *Notification*, *Alarm* or *Ringtone*.
- **Voice comes out of**: *Phone speaker* keeps directions on the phone while music plays over Bluetooth (not for Android Auto).
- **Pause the music for the voice**: stops the music instead of lowering it.
- **Play the voice like the chimes**: turn on if the music is lowered for the beeps but not for the voice.
- **Voice and language**: the system text-to-speech voice.
- **Volume**, **Chime volume** (beeps compared with the voice), **Voice boost** (louder than the music; turn it up a little at a time).

## While driving
*Road name, tunnels, media, locking*

- **Road name when driving without a route**, and **Heading next to the road name** ("Heading E").
- **Dim the map in tunnels**.
- **Fewer buttons while driving**.
- **Picture in picture**: on a phone, on a route, going to another app keeps the map, next turn and time left in a small window.
- **Clock on the map**: the time with the speedometer, handy when the status bar is covered.
- **Lock typing while driving**: voice search still works.
- **Media controls**: back, play / pause and next at the bottom of the screen while music plays, from any app.
- **Song and artwork**: shows what's playing (needs notification access; Google Play edition only).

## Places & trips
*Home, work, favourites, sharing, trip log*

- **Saved places**: set or change Home and Work.
- **Your places**: your own groups of places. **Add a place**, rename or remove them, and **Import Google Maps** (a *Saved Places.json* from Google Takeout).
- **Favourites**: **Sort favourites**, rename, **Move every favourite to Your places**, **Remove every favourite**.

**Under the search bar**
- **Search as a button**: with no route, a round search button in the corner instead of the search bar, so the arrow sits in the middle.
- **Home button**, **Work button**, **Your places next to Home and Work** (the starred ones), **Recent places under the search bar**.

**History and trips**
- **Recent destinations**: the places you've been to, also remembered for searching without signal.
- **Trip log**: your drives, with distance, moving time and top speed.

**My routes and GPX tracks**
- **My routes**: routes you've saved. **When a saved route fits**: offer it first, or start on it straight away.
- **GPX tracks**: tracks you've imported, to drive or show.

**Send places from your phone**
- Pair the head unit (**On the head unit** makes a code) with your phone (**On your phone**, type the code). Then *Send to car* on the phone's route screen sends a place to the car.

**Share your ETA / Places from other apps**
- **Send it to**: who gets your arrival time.
- **Google Maps links**: opens places shared from Google Maps and other apps.

## Fuel & charging
*Your fuel, prices, electric car*

**Fuel prices**
- **Your fuel**: unleaded, super, diesel, bioethanol (E85), LPG or electric.
- **Suggest a fuel stop on long trips**: over about 95 miles (150 km), the best-value station along the way, or for an EV the charging point with the shortest detour.
- **Prices**: UK prices need your own fuel-price **Client ID** and **Client secret**. **Spotted a wrong price?** reports one.
- **Fuel prices outside the UK**: **Tankerkönig key** (Germany), **FuelCheck API key** and **secret** (New South Wales and Tasmania).

**Your electric car**
- **Battery estimate on the trip card**: roughly how much battery you'll arrive with, worked out from your settings (not read from the car).
- **Show petrol stations too**: off, *Charging* takes the place of the Fuel button.
- **Charger speed**: hide chargers slower than 22, 50 or 150 kW (chargers of unknown speed still show).
- **Charger detour**: how many minutes off the route a charger can be and still be listed.
- **Only these providers**: names separated by commas; blank shows all.
- **Efficiency**, **Usable battery (kWh)** (60 if left blank), **Electricity price per kWh**.

**Fuel cost**
- **Fuel economy** / **Fuel use** and **Fuel price per litre**: for the fuel cost on the route options.
- **Live fuel cost**: what the trip has cost so far, under the trip card, and the total when you arrive.

## Offline maps
*Maps, routes and town names with no signal*

**Mapbox map with no signal**
- **Around home**: saves the 3D map within a set distance of Home.
- **Save the map for this route**, **Save every route's map** (in the background when a route starts), and **Save routes** on Wi-Fi only or always.

**Downloaded areas / Add an area**
- Download a country or region (**Area**) for offline routing, search, speed limits and cameras. Best on Wi-Fi: a country is usually 1 to 4 GB.
- **Update automatically**: newer map data, speed limits, cameras and town names download by themselves when you're not driving: *On Wi-Fi only* (the default), *Always* (mobile data too), or *Off* to be asked first.

**Without signal**
- **Offline mode**: nothing goes online (no traffic, weather or online search). Routes, search, limits and cameras come from your downloaded pack.
- **Which map to draw**: *Auto* keeps Mapbox's stored map and uses the downloaded pack's map only where Mapbox has nothing.
- **Towns and villages**: place names for searching offline.

## Screen & sizes
*Brightness, sizes, speed, card positions, units*

**Screen**
- **Full screen**: hides the status and navigation bars. Turn off if your head unit's own bar (climate, home button) gets covered.
- **Brightness**: how bright the screen is, like the phone's own slider. 100% leaves it to the system.
- **Dim the screen at night**: half as bright at night. With Brightness at 100% and auto-brightness on, it's left to the phone.
- **Keep the screen on**, and **Keep the screen on without a route** (on a phone it otherwise stays on only while you navigate).
- **Glass panels**: see-through cards tinted by the map. Off gives solid cards.
- **New portrait layout**: on an upright screen with no route, just the search bar at the top and your places, music and notices in a sheet along the bottom.
- **Mapbox logo position**: which corner Mapbox's required logo goes in.

**Sizes**
- **Automatically scale UI**: sizes everything from the screen's size and shape. Or set **Text and button size** yourself.
- **Advanced display sizes**: **Card size**, **Turn arrow size**, **Turn card width** and **height**, **Button size**, **Text size**, **Road name size**.

**Speed display**
- **Your speed next to the limit**, **Speedometer ring** (fills towards the limit, amber then red), **Big speed limit sign** (the limit big, your speed small), **Speed limit and speedometer size**.

**Card positions**
- Where the **Turn card**, **Trip card** and **Speedometer** go.

**Units**
- **Distance and speed** (miles or km), **Clock** (12 / 24-hour, or follow the device), **Temperature**.
- **Driving side**: right-hand drive (UK, cards on the left) or left-hand drive (US, Europe, screen mirrored). *Automatic* follows the country you're in.

## Android Auto
*Car-screen sizes and setting it up*

- **Button size**: the buttons on the trip card.
- **Cool running**: for phones that get hot. 30 frames a second, a flatter map and no rain or lamp effects. *Automatic* turns it on when the phone reports it's getting hot.
- **Search bar and places on the map**: off for a car with a rotary knob (Search, Home and Work move under Places ★). **Hide them after**: they come back when you touch the screen.
- **Sound button on the map**: sound on / alerts only / muted, when not navigating.
- **Microphone**: voice search on the car screen.
- **Phone battery on the car screen**: your phone's battery level in a small badge beside the speedometer. Off unless switched on.
- **Car data** › **Speed, battery and position**: lets Android Auto pass on the car's own speed, battery level and position. Without it, the app uses GPS and the battery % you set.

**Card positions on the car screen**
- **Trip card**, **Trip card in split screen**, **Speedometer**, **Speedometer style on the car screen** (*Compact* is a small dark pill with the limit and your speed side by side).

**Sizes on the car screen**
- **Overall size on the car screen**, **Speedometer and speed-limit sign size**, **Speed-limit sign size** (also the upcoming limit and the limit pop-up), and **Advanced car-screen sizes** (card, turn arrow, turn card width and height, buttons, bar height, text, navigation arrow, road name).

**Setting it up**
- **Android Auto settings** and **Android Auto app info** (to force stop it after changing its settings).
- **Not showing in Android Auto?** (GitHub edition): turn on *Unknown sources* in Android Auto's Developer settings, force stop Android Auto and reconnect, or install with the free AAEnabler app. Android Auto's own microphone may not reach apps from outside Google Play: use the app's search button instead.

## Performance
*Smoothness on slower head units, demo*

- **Map sharpness**: *Full*, *Balanced*, *Light* or *Lowest*. Lower draws fewer pixels, for a slow head unit.
- **Frame rate**: *Auto* starts at 30 and steps down when the head unit can't keep up, so the map moves evenly.
- **Fast map drawing**.
- **Performance meter**: a line at the top of the map with frames drawn, camera moves and pauses.
- **Demo drive button**: plays a route back so you can try the guidance while parked.
- **Show lane data diagnostics**: only in demo mode.

## Keys & accounts
*Map keys and search*

- **Search results**, and **Search as I type** (off waits until you press the search key).
- **Met Office API key**: optional, UK weather (free plan: 360 requests a day). **Test Met Office** checks it.
- **TomTom API key**: optional. The most accurate address search, and a backup for routes and traffic. Free from developer.tomtom.com.
- **Mapbox access token**: a public token starting `pk.` (never `sk.`). If the map reports 401, copy an active token from account.mapbox.com with styles:read and fonts:read.
- **Routes, search and traffic** › **Use**: which service, or *Auto*.

## About & backup
*Feedback, updates, permissions, backup*

- **Feedback or a problem**: sends a report with the app version, your unit and the last crash.
- **Donate** (GitHub edition): free, no ads; donations help keep it going.
- **Version**, **Check for updates** and **Look for updates by itself** (GitHub edition; at most every half hour, when there's a connection).
- **Setup**: go through the first-run steps again.
- **Permissions**: **Location** and **Location services**.
- **Backup**: save or restore **Settings, places and tracks**; **Include API keys** (the file is plain text, keep it safe); **Automatic backup** once a day to a folder you choose; **Backup files**: replace the last one, or keep the last 7.
- **Problem report**: after a crash, **This crash** sends it with your unit's details.
