# Horizon Maps privacy policy

Last updated: 17 November 2026

Horizon Maps is a navigation app for phones, car head units and Android Auto. This policy explains what information the app uses, where it goes, and the choices you have. It also applies to Horizon Nav, the same app published on GitHub.

## The short version

- There is no account and no sign-in.
- There are no adverts, and no advertising or tracking libraries.
- The developer does not run a server that stores your location, searches or trips.
- Your saved places, recent places, trip log, GPX tracks and settings are stored only on your device.
- To show maps and work out routes, the app sends your location and searches to the map and data services listed below.

## Information stored on your device

The app stores your home, work and saved places, recent searches and destinations, your trip log, imported GPX tracks and your settings. These stay on your device. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage, deletes them.

## Information sent to services the app uses

To work, the app sends requests to these services. A request contains only what is needed to answer it, such as a position, an area of the map, a route's start and end, or the words you searched for. Like any internet request, each service also sees your device's IP address.

- **Mapbox:** map images, search and routes. The map engine may also send Mapbox anonymous usage information; you can turn this off from the (i) button in the corner of the map. [Mapbox privacy policy](https://www.mapbox.com/legal/privacy)
- **TomTom:** routes, traffic and search. [TomTom privacy notice](https://www.tomtom.com/privacy/)
- **DGT (Spain):** when you are in Spain, the app downloads the DGT's public file of live traffic incidents (nap.dgt.es). It is the same file for everyone: no position or other information about you is sent.
- **OpenStreetMap services (Nominatim, Photon, Overpass):** place search, addresses, speed limits and road details near you.
- **Open-Meteo and RainViewer:** the weather and rain radar for your area.
- **Postcodes.io:** looking up UK postcodes you search for.
- **Fuel price services** (the UK government's Fuel Finder and similar official services in other countries): fuel prices near you or along your route, only when you use fuel features.
- **GeoNames:** town names for offline use, downloaded only when you choose offline maps.

## Optional features

- **Voice search:** when you tap the microphone, your speech is turned into text by your device's speech recognition service (usually Google's). The app receives only the text.
- **Send to car:** if you pair your phone with a head unit, places you send travel through ntfy.sh, a free message relay. They are scrambled with your pairing code, so only your paired devices can read them.
- **Now playing:** if you give the app notification access, it reads the song title and artist from your music app's notification to show them on the map. This stays on your device.
- **Sharing your ETA:** when you choose to share, the app hands a message to the app you pick. Nothing is sent unless you do this.
- **Reporting a problem:** the app can open a pre-filled GitHub issue in your browser. Nothing is sent unless you submit it yourself.

## Location

The app uses your precise location to show where you are, give directions and find places near you. While you navigate, it keeps using your location with a visible notification, so directions continue when the screen is off or another app is in front. It does not collect location when you are not using it or navigating.

## Purchases

If you subscribe, Google Play handles the payment. The developer does not receive your card details. The app checks with Google Play whether your subscription is active.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13.
